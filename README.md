# 1. GAME OVERVIEW

THE LAST SIGNAL is a science-fiction planetary survival and exploration game.

The player is a stranded astronaut on an enormous, mostly barren alien planet. The planet contains wildlife, natural resources, abandoned facilities and the remnants of an extremely advanced civilisation that became extinct hundreds of thousands of years ago.

Somewhere beneath the planet's surface, a mysterious signal continues transmitting.

The signal was activated after the civilisation's extinction event. It may be a quarantine mechanism, potentially designed to contain or prevent something from escaping.

The player gradually explores the planet, develops technology, establishes bases, discovers the planet's history and investigates the signal.

### Core experience

- Lonely exploration of an unfamiliar world.
- Moderate survival challenges.
- Resource collection and crafting.
- Modular base construction.
- Equipment and technology progression.
- Scientific discovery and documentation.
- Investigation of an ancient civilisation.
- A central mystery uncovered through exploration.

The atmosphere should emphasise isolation, discovery, curiosity and occasional danger.

# 2. WORLD DESIGN

## 2.1 The Planet

The game takes place on ONE enormous, continuous alien planet.

### Environmental characteristics

- Mostly barren and desolate.
- Unmistakably alien.
- Contains different geological and environmental regions.
- Contains natural resources and unusual formations.
- Has wildlife but no living intelligent inhabitants.
- Contains extensive remains of an ancient civilisation.

The world should feel enormous and largely unexplored.

## 2.2 Planet Generation

**Decision: Hybrid generation.**

The planet will combine procedural and handcrafted content.

- Procedurally generated terrain.
- Procedurally distributed resources and wildlife.
- Handcrafted landmarks and important locations.
- Handcrafted ancient ruins and facilities.
- Carefully designed environmental regions.
- Unique discoveries and points of interest.

The intention is to create a large world without sacrificing interesting locations or deliberate environmental storytelling.

## 2.3 Exploration Locations

Potential locations include:

- Ancient cities.
- Abandoned research facilities.
- Industrial complexes.
- Underground structures.
- Caves and subterranean networks.
- Geological formations.
- Crashed spacecraft.
- Abandoned settlements.
- Ancient transportation infrastructure.
- Enormous underground megastructures.

These are proposed location categories, not a final location list.

# 3. THE ANCIENT CIVILISATION

An intelligent civilisation once inhabited the planet.

### Established lore

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| Status | Extinct |
| Fate | Catastrophic extinction event |
| Age | Hundreds of thousands of years ago |
| Technology | Extremely advanced, far beyond humanity |
| Living inhabitants | None |

## 3.1 Their Remains

The civilisation should feel like a genuine, sophisticated society.

Its remains may include:

- Cities and settlements.
- Transportation networks.
- Scientific facilities.
- Industrial facilities.
- Residential structures.
- Energy infrastructure.
- Manufacturing complexes.
- Underground facilities.
- Advanced machinery.
- Historical archives.

Some technology may still function despite the civilisation having disappeared hundreds of thousands of years ago.

The architecture and infrastructure should feel interconnected rather than consisting of random alien structures.

## 3.2 The Extinction Event

The civilisation suffered a catastrophic extinction event.

The exact cause has not yet been established.

The event is a central part of the planet's history.

# 4. THE MYSTERIOUS SIGNAL

The central mystery of the game.

### Established decisions

- The signal is of unknown origin.
- It originates somewhere on the planet.
- Its source may be deep underground.
- It was activated AFTER the civilisation's extinction.
- It may be a quarantine signal.

### Possible interpretation

The signal could be an automated quarantine mechanism.

Potential purposes include:

- Containing something.
- Preventing something from escaping.
- Warning against an unknown threat.
- Maintaining an automated protective system.
- Preserving a containment boundary.

These are possibilities, not confirmed explanations.

The player should gradually discover evidence and form theories.

The mystery should not be explained immediately.

# 5. THE PLAYER AND FREIGHTER

The player is a stranded astronaut.

Their spacecraft was a massive interstellar freighter.

## 5.1 The Freighter

The freighter is severely damaged.

### Established characteristics

- Large, somewhere between moderately sized and enormous.
- Severely damaged.
- Extensively lootable.
- Contains recoverable resources and equipment.
- Acts as an explorable location.
- The player can travel to it.

It is NOT established as:

- The player's permanent base.
- A repairable spacecraft.
- A flyable spacecraft.
- The centre of the player's progression.

The freighter should contain interesting areas such as cargo storage, machinery, maintenance spaces, control rooms and other specialised compartments.

Its exact layout and contents remain to be designed.

# 6. SURVIVAL SYSTEM

**Difficulty: Moderate.**

Survival should create meaningful decisions without constantly interrupting exploration.

## 6.1 Survival Requirements

| System | Function |
|---|---|
| Oxygen | Required for breathing |
| Hydration | Water requirements |
| Hunger | Food requirements |
| Temperature | Protection against environmental conditions |
| Energy | Suit and equipment power |
| Radiation | Radiation exposure management |
| Shield | Suit integrity and protection |

The shield represents the integrity/protection of the player's suit.

These systems should have different interactions and purposes.

## 6.2 Death and Respawning

When the player dies:

- They lose some carried resources.
- They respawn at their starting pod.
- If they have established a base, they can respawn there instead.

Bases therefore have a practical survival function beyond storage and construction.

# 7. PLAYER EQUIPMENT

## 7.1 The Multitool

The multitool is the player's primary general-purpose piece of equipment.

It combines multiple functions.

| Function | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Scanning | Identify and analyse objects |
| Mining | Extract natural resources |
| Defence | Protect against dangerous wildlife |
| Utility | Interact with machinery |
| Salvaging | Recover components and materials |

The multitool is upgradeable.

Upgrades can improve existing capabilities and potentially unlock additional functionality.

## 7.2 Electrical Power

All electrical equipment requires a power source.

Power management is an important part of equipment design.

Equipment may use the player's suit battery or compatible external power sources, depending on its design.
