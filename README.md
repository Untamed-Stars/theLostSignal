# THE LAST SIGNAL
## Game Design Document & Project Reference

**Version:** 0.1  
**Last updated:** 14 November 2026  
**Status:** Concept development  
**Genre:** Science-fiction survival, crafting, exploration and mystery

---

# 1. GAME OVERVIEW

THE LAST SIGNAL is a science-fiction planetary survival and exploration game.

The player is a stranded astronaut on an enormous, mostly barren alien planet. The planet contains wildlife, natural resources, abandoned facilities and the remnants of an extremely advanced civilisation that became extinct hundreds of thousands of years ago.

Somewhere beneath the planet's surface, a mysterious signal continues transmitting.

The signal was activated after the civilisation's extinction event. It may be a quarantine mechanism, potentially designed to contain or prevent something from escaping.

The player gradually explores the planet, develops technology, establishes bases, discovers the planet's history and investigates the signal.

### Core experience

- Lonely exploration of an unfamiliar world.
- Moderate survival challenges.
- Resource collection and crafting.
- Modular base construction.
- Equipment and technology progression.
- Scientific discovery and documentation.
- Investigation of an ancient civilisation.
- A central mystery uncovered through exploration.

The atmosphere should emphasise isolation, discovery, curiosity and occasional danger.

# 2. WORLD DESIGN

## 2.1 The Planet

The game takes place on ONE enormous, continuous alien planet.

### Environmental characteristics

- Mostly barren and desolate.
- Unmistakably alien.
- Contains different geological and environmental regions.
- Contains natural resources and unusual formations.
- Has wildlife but no living intelligent inhabitants.
- Contains extensive remains of an ancient civilisation.

The world should feel enormous and largely unexplored.

## 2.2 Planet Generation

**Decision: Hybrid generation.**

The planet will combine procedural and handcrafted content.

- Procedurally generated terrain.
- Procedurally distributed resources and wildlife.
- Handcrafted landmarks and important locations.
- Handcrafted ancient ruins and facilities.
- Carefully designed environmental regions.
- Unique discoveries and points of interest.

The intention is to create a large world without sacrificing interesting locations or deliberate environmental storytelling.

## 2.3 Exploration Locations

Potential locations include:

- Ancient cities.
- Abandoned research facilities.
- Industrial complexes.
- Underground structures.
- Caves and subterranean networks.
- Geological formations.
- Crashed spacecraft.
- Abandoned settlements.
- Ancient transportation infrastructure.
- Enormous underground megastructures.

These are proposed location categories, not a final location list.

# 3. THE ANCIENT CIVILISATION

An intelligent civilisation once inhabited the planet.

### Established lore

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| Status | Extinct |
| Fate | Catastrophic extinction event |
| Age | Hundreds of thousands of years ago |
| Technology | Extremely advanced, far beyond humanity |
| Living inhabitants | None |

## 3.1 Their Remains

The civilisation should feel like a genuine, sophisticated society.

Its remains may include:

- Cities and settlements.
- Transportation networks.
- Scientific facilities.
- Industrial facilities.
- Residential structures.
- Energy infrastructure.
- Manufacturing complexes.
- Underground facilities.
- Advanced machinery.
- Historical archives.

Some technology may still function despite the civilisation having disappeared hundreds of thousands of years ago.

The architecture and infrastructure should feel interconnected rather than consisting of random alien structures.

## 3.2 The Extinction Event

The civilisation suffered a catastrophic extinction event.

The exact cause has not yet been established.

The event is a central part of the planet's history.

---

# 4. THE MYSTERIOUS SIGNAL

The central mystery of the game.

### Established decisions

- The signal is of unknown origin.
- It originates somewhere on the planet.
- Its source may be deep underground.
- It was activated AFTER the civilisation's extinction.
- It may be a quarantine signal.

### Possible interpretation

The signal could be an automated quarantine mechanism.

Potential purposes include:

- Containing something.
- Preventing something from escaping.
- Warning against an unknown threat.
- Maintaining an automated protective system.
- Preserving a containment boundary.

These are possibilities, not confirmed explanations.

The player should gradually discover evidence and form theories.

The mystery should not be explained immediately.

---

# 5. THE PLAYER AND FREIGHTER

The player is a stranded astronaut.

Their spacecraft was a massive interstellar freighter.

## 5.1 The Freighter

The freighter is severely damaged.

### Established characteristics

- Large, somewhere between moderately sized and enormous.
- Severely damaged.
- Extensively lootable.
- Contains recoverable resources and equipment.
- Acts as an explorable location.
- The player can travel to it.

It is NOT established as:

- The player's permanent base.
- A repairable spacecraft.
- A flyable spacecraft.
- The centre of the player's progression.

The freighter should contain interesting areas such as cargo storage, machinery, maintenance spaces, control rooms and other specialised compartments.

Its exact layout and contents remain to be designed.

# 6. SURVIVAL SYSTEM

**Difficulty: Moderate.**

Survival should create meaningful decisions without constantly interrupting exploration.

## 6.1 Survival Requirements

| System | Function |
|---|---|
| Oxygen | Required for breathing |
| Hydration | Water requirements |
| Hunger | Food requirements |
| Temperature | Protection against environmental conditions |
| Energy | Suit and equipment power |
| Radiation | Radiation exposure management |
| Shield | Suit integrity and protection |

The shield represents the integrity/protection of the player's suit.

These systems should have different interactions and purposes.

## 6.2 Death and Respawning

When the player dies:

- They lose some carried resources.
- They respawn at their starting pod.
- If they have established a base, they can respawn there instead.

Bases therefore have a practical survival function beyond storage and construction.

# 7. PLAYER EQUIPMENT

## 7.1 The Multitool

The multitool is the player's primary general-purpose piece of equipment.

It combines multiple functions.

| Function | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Scanning | Identify and analyse objects |
| Mining | Extract natural resources |
| Defence | Protect against dangerous wildlife |
| Utility | Interact with machinery |
| Salvaging | Recover components and materials |

The multitool is upgradeable.

Upgrades can improve existing capabilities and potentially unlock additional functionality.

## 7.2 Electrical Power

All electrical equipment requires a power source.

Power management is an important part of equipment design.

Equipment may use the player's suit battery or compatible external power sources, depending on its design.

## 7.3 Jetpack

The player has a rechargeable jetpack.

Established mechanics:

- Recharges automatically while the player is on the ground.
- Uses the suit's battery to recharge.
- Allows additional mobility during exploration.

Jetpack capacity and recharge performance may eventually be upgradeable.

---

# 8. VEHICLE SYSTEM

Vehicles use a modular platform.

Rather than creating completely separate vehicle systems, the player has a common vehicle base to which different chassis can be attached.

## 8.1 Chassis Types

| Chassis | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Land | Ground exploration |
| Air | Aerial exploration |
| Underwater | Submerged exploration |
| Boat | Surface water travel |

The vehicle platform can potentially support additional modules, equipment and upgrades.

Vehicle construction, fabrication and customisation will need further design.

---

# 9. SCIENTIFIC DISCOVERY

The player can scan and document discoveries throughout the planet.

The scientific database becomes a record of the player's expedition.

## 9.1 Scan Categories

### Wildlife
- Discover animal species.
- Record characteristics.
- Document behaviours.

### Flora
- Identify plant species.
- Record properties.
- Discover useful biological resources.

### Equipment fragments
- Discover fragments of damaged equipment.
- Recover information about corrupted fabrication blueprints.
- Reconstruct lost technology.

### Alien artefacts
- Analyse ancient objects.
- Investigate their purpose.
- Discover historical information.

### Other discoveries
Additional categories may include minerals, geological formations, unusual phenomena and ancient structures.

Not every scan needs to provide a direct gameplay reward.

Scientific discovery should also contribute to understanding the planet.

# 10. LOGS AND STORYTELLING

Logs are an important storytelling mechanism.

They provide information about the planet, its history, abandoned locations and the ancient civilisation.

## 10.1 Log Types

| Type | Contents |
|---|---|
| Crewmember logs | Personal recordings and experiences |
| Area scans | Geographical and environmental information |
| Research logs | Scientific investigations |
| Historical records | Ancient civilisation history |
| Personal records | Diaries and correspondence |
| System logs | Automated machinery and facility records |
| Signal recordings | Transmissions and unusual phenomena |

## 10.2 Discovery Methods

Logs may be recovered through:

- Physical data drives.
- Recording devices.
- Computer terminals.
- Abandoned facilities.
- Damaged equipment.
- Ancient archives.
- Suit recordings and environmental scans.

## 10.3 Expedition Database

A personal database should organise the player's discoveries.

Potential sections:

- Biological records.
- Geological records.
- Artificial structures.
- Historical archives.
- Personal recordings.
- Signal investigations.

The database should become a comprehensive record of the player's exploration.

# 11. CRAFTING AND FABRICATION

## 11.1 Blueprint Recovery

**Decision: Recover fragments and reconstruct blueprints.**

The player discovers fragmented fabrication data throughout the planet.

Equipment fragments can be scanned and analysed to recover corrupted blueprint information.

Reconstructed blueprints unlock fabrication recipes.

## 11.2 Fabrication Stations

**Decision: Dedicated fabrication stations.**

Fabrication takes place at specialised stations.

Potential station categories:

- General equipment fabrication.
- Suit component fabrication.
- Multitool fabrication and upgrades.
- Vehicle component fabrication.
- Structural component fabrication.
- Advanced technology fabrication.

These categories are proposed and can be refined later.

## 11.3 Material Processing

Different recipes have different requirements.

Some use raw resources directly.

Others require refined materials or manufactured components.

This creates a resource-processing progression system.

# 12. BASE BUILDING

**Decision: Fully modular construction using individual components.**

The player can build highly customisable bases.

Construction should not be restricted to a small selection of predefined buildings.

Potential components include:

- Structural foundations.
- Floors.
- Walls.
- Ceilings.
- Doors.
- Windows.
- Storage.
- Fabrication stations.
- Research equipment.
- Power generators.
- Lighting.
- Vehicle facilities.

These are proposed component categories.

## 12.1 Power Infrastructure

Bases require functional power systems.

Multiple power technologies will exist, including advanced technologies discovered during exploration.

Potential power sources include different generator technologies.

Power infrastructure should support actual base functionality rather than being purely decorative.

## 12.2 Base Functionality

Bases should support:

- Storage.
- Fabrication.
- Research.
- Power distribution.
- Equipment management.
- Vehicle facilities.
- Survival and recovery.

The player should be able to create elaborate facilities with practical uses.

# 13. GAMEPLAY LOOP

The established primary gameplay loop is:

1. Explore the planet.
2. Discover locations, wildlife, flora and resources.
3. Scan and document discoveries.
4. Collect natural resources and technological salvage.
5. Recover blueprint fragments.
6. Reconstruct fabrication blueprints.
7. Manufacture equipment and components.
8. Upgrade equipment and improve survival capabilities.
9. Construct and expand bases.
10. Explore increasingly challenging or inaccessible locations.
11. Discover more about the ancient civilisation.
12. Investigate the mysterious signal.

These activities should reinforce one another.

For example, discovering an ancient facility might reveal a blueprint fragment that unlocks technology needed to explore a previously inaccessible region.

# 14. STORY STRUCTURE

The following is a PROPOSED progression structure, not a fixed mission list.

### Phase 1: The Surface
- Establish survival.
- Explore the surrounding environment.
- Discover the first ancient ruins.
- Recover useful materials and technology.

### Phase 2: The Remnants
- Explore abandoned facilities.
- Discover historical logs.
- Understand the civilisation's technological capabilities.
- Find evidence of the extinction event.

### Phase 3: The Signal
- Investigate increasingly unusual discoveries.
- Find evidence of an underground structure.
- Learn more about the signal's activation.

### Phase 4: The Truth
- Reach the signal's source.
- Investigate the quarantine mechanism.
- Uncover the circumstances surrounding its activation.

The exact story, ending and final revelations remain undecided.

# 15. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

The following principles should guide development.

1. **Exploration should be rewarding.** Discoveries should provide information, resources, technology or meaningful experiences.

2. **The world should feel enormous.** Even with one planet, its environments and locations should offer substantial variety.

3. **Systems should connect.** Survival, equipment, crafting, construction and exploration should reinforce one another.

4. **Technology should be discovered.** Progression should involve investigation and reconstruction, not simply arbitrary level unlocks.

5. **The civilisation should feel real.** Its remains should communicate its history, infrastructure and technological sophistication.

6. **The mystery should develop naturally.** Avoid explaining everything through exposition or quest markers.

7. **Survival should be moderate.** It should create interesting decisions without overwhelming exploration.

8. **Bases should be functional and customisable.** Construction should have meaningful gameplay applications.

9. **The player should feel isolated.** The absence of living intelligent inhabitants is an important part of the atmosphere.

10. **The game should have its own identity.** It should not simply be a clone of another space exploration or survival game.
